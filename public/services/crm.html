Questions

Good to know

Which CRM should I use?

It depends on your business and budget. I set up GoHighLevel and other CRMs, and we can talk through which one fits what you need.

What’s the difference between CRM setup and a custom CRM?

CRM setup configures an existing product like GoHighLevel for your business. A custom CRM is software built from scratch for you, which makes sense when your process doesn’t fit what’s already out there.

Can my website send leads into my CRM?

That’s the goal. If you need a new site, see website development, and I’ll plan the two together.
