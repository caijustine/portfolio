Local SEO & Google Business Profile services

When someone nearby searches for what you do, your Google Business Profile and your website decide whether they find you. Managing Google Business Profiles is my day job, and I bring that to small businesses in St. George, Utah and beyond.

Who it’s for

Is this you?

  • You don’t have a Google Business Profile yet, or you never finished setting one up
  • Your profile has old hours, missing photos, or information that’s out of date
  • You serve customers across an area, like a cleaner or contractor, and want to show up where you work
  • You don’t have time to keep your profile active
Google Business Profile

Setup

  • Creating or claiming your profile, and walking you through Google’s verification
  • Choosing the right business categories
  • Filling in your services, hours, service area, and business description
  • Adding photos of your work, team, or storefront
  • Linking your profile and your website so they point to each other

Google Business Profile setup is quoted per business.

Ongoing

Management

A profile works best when it stays active. Monthly care ($80/month) includes regular Google Business Profile posts and a monthly SEO check-in, and full tech management includes Google Business Profile setup.

Your website

Local SEO basics

  • A unique page title and description on every page
  • Clear headings that describe what each page is about
  • Your business name, address, and phone number consistent across your site and your profile
  • Structured data that tells Google what kind of business you are and where you work
  • A sitemap so Google can find every page

SEO foundations are built into every website I build through website development, and they’re also available on their own as SEO basics. For an example, see the city and service pages on Rose’s Cleaning.

Straight talk

No ranking promises

Nobody can guarantee a top spot on Google, and I won’t promise one. What I can do is make sure your profile and website are complete, accurate, and set up the way Google expects, which is the foundation local search is built on.
